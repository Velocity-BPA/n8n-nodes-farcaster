# Automate Your Farcaster Presence with Our New n8n Node

We're excited to announce the release of n8n-nodes-farcaster, a comprehensive community node that brings Farcaster's decentralized social protocol directly into your n8n workflows!

## Why We Built This

Farcaster is rapidly growing as a decentralized social network, but managing your presence there manually can be time-consuming. Whether you're a developer building Frames, a community manager scheduling casts, or a brand engaging across multiple channels, you need automation to scale your efforts.

That's why Velocity BPA created n8n-nodes-farcaster—to give you programmatic control over your Farcaster activities without writing custom code.

## What Can You Do With It?

Our node packs powerful features that cover the full Farcaster ecosystem:

- **Post and Manage Casts**: Automatically publish casts based on triggers like RSS feeds, database updates, or scheduled times
- **Channel Operations**: Monitor and interact with Farcaster channels programmatically
- **Frames Support**: Build and deploy interactive Frames with your existing n8n workflows
- **SIWF Authentication**: Implement Sign-In With Farcaster for seamless user authentication
- **Ed25519 Signing**: Handle cryptographic operations securely within your workflows

Imagine automatically cross-posting your blog content to Farcaster, sending personalized casts to new followers, or building sophisticated Frame-based applications—all through n8n's visual interface.

## Getting Started

Installation is straightforward. Simply run:


npm install n8n-nodes-farcaster


Then restart your n8n instance, and you'll find the Farcaster node available in your node palette.
